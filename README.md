# Aura Luxe — Luxury Hotels, Private Villas & Curated Concierge Services

**Aura Luxe** is an ultra-premium, interactive hospitality booking platform designed for reserving 5-star private villas, alpine chalets, penthouses, private aviation charters, superyachts, and in-residence Michelin dining.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Curated Global Stays & VIP Services
- **Bora Bora Overwater Sanctuary** (Lagoon infinity pool, glass viewing floor)
- **Matterhorn Royal Alpine Chalet** in Zermatt (Ski-in/Ski-out, sauna, roaring fireplace)
- **Santorini Caldera Cliffside Suite** in Oia (Volcanic cave plunge pool, caldera sunset)
- **Palais Montaigne Royal Penthouse** in Paris (Avenue Montaigne, Eiffel Tower views)
- **Imperial Arashiyama Heritage Ryokan** in Kyoto (Private thermal onsen & zen garden)
- **Villa Di Bellosguardo Wine Estate** in Tuscany (Renaissance estate with Chianti vineyard)
- **Aurelia Superyacht Charter** in Monaco & the French Riviera (115ft tri-deck, crew of 6)
- **Gulfstream G650 Executive Jet** (Intercontinental transcontinental private aviation)
- **3-Star Michelin Chef & Sommelier** (Bespoke 7-course private tasting in-suite)
- **Holistic Alpine Thermal Spa** in St. Moritz (Cryotherapy & longevity wellness)
- **Ferrari 296 GTB Supercar Chauffeur** in Milan & Lake Como

---

### 2. Smart Booking & Discovery Engine
- **Multi-Filter Search Dock**: Filter by destination keyword, check-in & check-out dates, and party size.
- **Category Tabs**: Switch between All Curations, Private Villas, Alpine Chalets, City Penthouses, Aviation & Yachts, Michelin Dining, and Wellness Spas.
- **Live Price Range Slider**: Adjust price threshold up to $10,000 with real-time grid update.
- **Amenity Filters**: Filter instantly by Private Pool, Oceanfront, Ski-in/Ski-out, Helipad, Private Butler, Michelin Chef, and Wellness Spa.
- **Dynamic Sorting**: Sort by Curator's Choice, Highest Star Rating, Price: Low to High, or Price: High to Low.

---

### 3. Interactive Detail & Checkout Modal
- **High-Resolution Visual Showcase**: Cinematic generated imagery with detailed specifications (guests, beds, baths, square footage).
- **Interactive Add-On Services**: Toggle airport VIP catamaran transfers, Dom Pérignon & caviar welcome packages, in-suite couples massages, heliskiing passes, and sommelier tastings.
- **Live Price Breakdown**: Real-time recalculation of base nights/sessions, add-on totals, 12% luxury hospitality tax, and VIP privilege discounts.
- **Promo Code Engine**:
  - `LUXE2026`: 15% VIP Privileges discount
  - `HORIZON`: 20% Founder Circle discount
  - `AURA100`: 10% Welcome Credit
- **Instant Booking Confirmation**: Generates unique reservation codes (e.g. `AUR-89241`), confirms guest details, and provides printable vouchers.

---

### 4. Client Account & Management Drawers
- **Saved Wishlist Drawer**: Click heart icons to bookmark favorite properties with persistent local storage.
- **"My Reservations" Drawer**: Review active and upcoming bookings, status badges, guest details, and one-click cancellation.
- **Multi-Currency Converter**: Real-time conversions across **USD ($)**, **EUR (€)**, **GBP (£)**, **JPY (¥)**, and **AED (د.إ)**.
- **Theme Switcher**: Seamless toggle between **Obsidian Dark Luxury** and **Alabaster Pearl Light** modes.

---

### 5. 👑 Owner Studio & Platform Management Portal
- **⚡ Owner Studio**: Accessible via the header navbar, the floating bottom-left pill, or by pressing `Alt + A`.
- **🏷️ Real-Time Price & Inventory Control**:
  - Direct inline price adjustments on every property and service with instant `localStorage` persistence.
  - Mass price adjustments (`+5%`, `+10%`, `-5%`, `-10%`).
  - Add custom new luxury properties or services with title, image, pricing, specs, and descriptions.
  - One-click **"Export Catalog (JSON)"** and **"↺ Reset Defaults"**.
  - **Live Card Edit Mode**: When enabled, glowing golden `[✏️ $X Edit]` badges appear directly on catalog cards for 1-click price changes.
- **💳 Multi-Gateway Payment Configuration**:
  - **Credit & Debit Cards (Stripe)**: Toggle live/test mode, configure publishable & secret keys, test mode autofill.
  - **PayPal**: Business receiving account and Client ID setup.
  - **Apple Pay & Google Pay**: 1-click biometric checkout configuration.
  - **VIP Direct Bank Wire / Escrow**: Configurable bank coordinates (Bank Name, Beneficiary, IBAN, SWIFT, Escrow instructions).
  - **Cryptocurrency (USDT / BTC / ETH)**: Configure Web3 wallet addresses with 1-click address copy.
  - **Custom Platform Taxes**: Adjustable Luxury Tourism Tax rate (0% to 40%).
- **📊 Sales & Bookings Ledger**:
  - Live revenue analytics (Total Volume, Confirmed Orders, Escrow Pending).
  - Transaction records with unique IDs (`TXN-ST-...`, `TXN-PP-...`, `TXN-WIRE-...`), guest details, and payment statuses.
  - One-click CSV export of the financial ledger.

---

### 6. 24/7 AI Luxury Concierge Chat Widget
- Floating interactive concierge assistant ("Aura Concierge AI") that answers questions and provides direct links to romantic stays, ski chalets, private aviation quotes, yachts, and Michelin chef experiences.
